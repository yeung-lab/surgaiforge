Why SurgAI Forge exists

Surgical AI progresses faster when the work is shared.

01

Research is fragmented

Models, data, and annotations are often developed independently and are difficult to reuse.

02

Clinical adoption is difficult

Useful research models rarely become practical tools that clinicians can test and use.

03

Technical barriers remain high

Most surgeons cannot easily run, adapt, or train surgical AI models on their own.

How the ecosystem works

Every contribution makes the next model better.

Clinical use and technical development reinforce each other through a shared set of data, models, and evaluation tools.
