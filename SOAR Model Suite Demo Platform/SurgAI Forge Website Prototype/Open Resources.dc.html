SurgAI Forge includes two downloadable applications that let you work with surgical AI models on your own computer.

Surgical Objective Analysis and Review

A desktop application for reviewing surgical video alongside AI-generated outputs. Individual surgeons can build and revisit their own video libraries, while institutions can create shared libraries for teaching, review, and collaboration.
