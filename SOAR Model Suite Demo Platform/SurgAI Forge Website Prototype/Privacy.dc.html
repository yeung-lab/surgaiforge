Privacy Policy

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the SurgAI Forge website (the “Site”). It does not cover information you provide to third-party platforms linked from the Site (for example, external code repositories, publication servers, or video hosts), which are governed by their own privacy policies.

This policy does not address clinical data or research datasets used by SurgAI Forge for model development; those are governed by the data-use agreements, institutional review approvals, and contracts under which the data were obtained.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Mailing-list subscriptions. If you subscribe to updates, we collect your email address and any name or affiliation you choose to provide.
  • Access requests. If you request access to a Resource, we collect the information you submit in that request — which may include your name, email, affiliation, role, and the purpose of your request.
  • Correspondence. If you contact us, we keep a record of the communication and the contact details you provide.

Information collected automatically

  • Server and device information. Like most websites, our hosting providers may automatically log basic technical information when you visit, including IP address, approximate location, browser type and version, referring page, and the pages you request. These logs are used to operate and secure the Site.
  • Local storage. The Site may use local storage in your browser to remember non-identifying preferences (for example, the last view you selected in a demonstration).

3. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • operate, maintain, and improve the Site;
  • respond to requests for access, information, or support;
  • send updates about SurgAI Forge Resources if you have subscribed;
  • protect the Site against misuse, fraud, or security incidents; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Cookies & analytics

The Site aims to minimize the use of cookies. Where cookies or similar technologies are used, they are limited to what is necessary to operate the Site (for example, security and basic preferences). If we introduce analytics in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, provide controls to opt out.

5. Model demonstrations

Interactive demonstrations on the Site display precomputed model outputs rendered in your browser. Unless expressly stated otherwise, no inference is performed in your browser, and no video or data you provide is transmitted to us as part of the demonstration.

6. Sharing of information

We share personal information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With service providers that help us operate the Site (such as hosting and email delivery), under obligations to use the information only to provide services to us;
  • With program collaborators when necessary to evaluate an access request or coordinate a response to an inquiry, and only to the extent needed for that purpose;
  • When required by law, such as to comply with a valid legal process or to protect the rights, property, or safety of SurgAI Forge, our users, or the public; or
  • With your consent, or at your direction.

7. Third-party services

The Site loads a small number of third-party resources, which may include web fonts and similar assets. These providers may receive your IP address and basic request information as part of serving those resources. We do not share additional personal information with them. Pages that embed third-party content (for example, video or map embeds, where present) are governed by the privacy policies of those providers.

8. Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or de-identify it.

9. Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage, however, is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information you provide.

10. Your choices & rights

You may unsubscribe from our mailing list at any time using the link in our emails or by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights — such as to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise a right, contact us at the address below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

11. International transfers

SurgAI Forge collaborators and service providers may be located in different countries. Where personal information is transferred across borders, we rely on appropriate legal mechanisms and safeguards to protect that information.

12. Children

The Site is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. If we make material changes, we will take reasonable steps to notify you.

14. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy may be sent to surgaiforge@gmail.com.
